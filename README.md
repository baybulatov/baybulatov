# I'm Rustam – Principal & Product Designer 👋

**Principal & Product Designer with a business mindset.** Based in Belgrade, Serbia · open to relocation and remote.

I design mobile, web and SaaS products and take them from zero to production: research, MVP scope, design systems, A/B tests, handoff. 40+ products shipped across wellness, fintech, CRM, eCommerce, blockchain, AI and edtech. I care about design that moves a business metric, and I measure it.

## Selected results

- Rebranded and redesigned **OGCommunity**: audience grew from 10k to 500k DAU, 2.5M total users.
- Ran A/B tests on paywalls and App Store screenshots: paywall conversion 3% → 8%, install conversion 4% → 7%.
- Built the foundation of an **Atomic Design System** at innoscripta GmbH (CRM, 6 designers): UI design speed +50%.
- Founding designer of **Trackerus**: MVP on Google Play and App Store, site on Framer nominated for Best Website at WDAwards, Product of the Day on Designer Daily Report.
- Led a cross-functional team of 12 (design, frontend, backend, ML, SMM) on a mobile wellness product.
